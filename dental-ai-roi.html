4  ·  Honest limitations

  • These are illustrative estimates, not predictions. Your actual missed-call rate, booking rate, and patient values will differ. Verify with your own phone logs before making any purchasing decision.
  • AI agents don't replace your front desk. They handle after-hours and overflow calls well (scheduling, FAQs, reminders), but insurance verification edge cases, anxious patients, and complex treatment discussions still need humans. Most practices deploy AI as overflow + after-hours coverage, not replacement.
  • Healthcare compliance matters. If the agent handles protected health information, you need a vendor willing to sign a HIPAA Business Associate Agreement (BAA). Confirm this before buying, as not all voice-AI platforms offer one.
  • Recovery rate is the swing variable. An AI agent that answers instantly still can't book a caller who was price-shopping three competitors. We default to 60%, though many practices will see less.
  • Costs shown are platform usage only. DIY platforms need setup time (typically 2–5 days of configuration); managed services ($300–$2,000/mo) include setup and maintenance but vary widely by vendor.
  • We are not affiliated with any vendor named here. Pricing changes frequently, so verify current pricing on vendor sites before deciding.
